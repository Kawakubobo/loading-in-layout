# Thinking and progress orbits

Use concentric circular text for thinking, source exploration, and progress reports.
Each ring represents an active workstream. Repeat its label with middle-dot separators to fill the circumference.

- Light workload: two compact rings, approximately three grid columns.
- Multiple workstreams: four rings, approximately four to six grid columns.
- Reveal inner to outer with hard cuts, 120–180 ms apart.
- Continue slow rotation with gentle, staggered speed pulses. Never stop or bounce the scale.
- Dismiss outer to inner with hard cuts. No fade.
- Keep existing typography and canvas rules.
- Demo labels are samples; live labels must come from actual published activity.

The 10-second demo renderer is scripts/render-orbits-demo.swift.
Scenes at 0–1.33s and 6–8s contain typography only, including circular text.
The exported video contains only the 1920×1080 white canvas.
